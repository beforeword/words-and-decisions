## S1 · Definition: AI and GPAI

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 · Article 3(1), 3(63)

EU regulation; two provisions in its definitions section are examined. Point 1 links inputs and outputs. Point 63 links the GPAI category to performing a broad range of tasks and integration into different applications.

<https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-3>

**Source excerpt**

> “infers” (Article 3(1)); “significant generality” (Article 3(63))

**beforeword commentary**

'System', 'infers', 'autonomy', 'model' and 'generality' enter as written terms. Combining them in a definition specifies a reading for this document. Which records and comparison procedure are used to assign this category? How is a result on selected tasks used to support a broader attribution? Show the category wording, tasks, results, threshold and rule used for the transition. 'Task', 'result' and 'rule' are also records here: presenting them makes the assigned reading available for examination. These provisions specify terms for the regulation. They do not require anyone to identify themselves with learned words. GPAI and AGI cannot be silently interchanged; the word 'law' does not complete the examination of an attribution.

## S2 · Oversight: what the word 'trust' adds

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 · Article 14, particularly 14(4)(b–d)

EU regulation; an article on oversight of systems classified as high-risk. The article addresses automatic or excessive reliance on outputs, their interpretation, and the option to disregard or reverse an output.

<https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-14>

**Source excerpt**

> “automation bias” (Article 14(4)(b))

**beforeword commentary**

'Human', 'system', 'risk' and 'oversight' are written here. 'Trust' is an additional word in this discussion, not a state presented for inspection or a quotation from the article. Which record is read as a basis for action? Who assigns that reading, under which rule? What is added when this transition is described as 'trust'? Keep the output separate from its interpretation, the decision and the stated permission to act. 'Decision', 'permission' and 'basis' also require the corresponding records and their use to be shown. The article already addresses over-reliance; that provision cannot be presented as a missing safeguard. The beforeword proposal concerns making a particular transition from record to attribution and action visible.

## S3 · Autonomy: where involvement is bounded

Explanatory memorandum on the updated OECD definition of an AI system · Printed page 6: Role of humans, autonomy and adaptiveness

An OECD memorandum explaining a definition in a recommendation; not a standalone law. The memorandum describes autonomy through the degree of learning or action without human involvement after delegation and automation.

<https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/03/explanatory-memorandum-on-the-updated-oecd-definition-of-an-ai-system_3c815e51/623da898-en.pdf>

**Source excerpt**

> “without human involvement” (Printed page 6)

**beforeword commentary**

'Autonomy', 'system', 'learning', 'action' and 'human' first enter as words. The phrase about the absence of involvement assigns a description; it does not present what is named in place of the record. Which part of the operation is called autonomous? Which records are used to distinguish involvement from its absence? On what grounds does the selected part describe the whole system? State the bounds of the operation, its starting conditions, available interventions and execution records. 'Involvement' and 'absence' remain terms in this account; how they are applied must be available for examination. This is the term's use in the memorandum. It does not present consciousness, will or an independent 'I', and it does not define human self-description.

## S4 · AGI: how 'outperformance' is written

OpenAI Charter · Opening mission paragraph, definition attached to AGI

An organisation's charter setting out its principles; neither a law nor a single international definition. The charter describes AGI through high autonomy and outperformance across most economically valuable work.

<https://openai.com/charter/>

**Source excerpt**

> “outperform humans at most economically valuable work” (Opening mission paragraph)

**beforeword commentary**

'AGI', 'system', 'human', 'outperformance', 'work' and 'value' enter as written forms. The comparison already uses selected readings of these words. Who selects the work, measures and threshold? Which records receive the labels 'human' and 'system'? How does the result of that comparison come to be read as 'AGI'? Publish the comparisons, conditions, scales and rule for assigning the label. 'Outperforms' must be accompanied by the chosen comparison procedure; repeating the word does not present that procedure. The wording belongs to this charter. It does not establish a general criterion for consciousness or understanding. In this discussion, too, 'criterion', 'consciousness' and 'understanding' remain words.
